    Arrange your vocal layers

    1. Select a vocals-only audio file in the library. 2. Add harmonies or choose separately recorded parts. 3. Render the mix, listen, then use Effects for reverb and Edit → Save as to export.

    These are starting settings inspired by common production techniques, not artist presets. Use your original vocal as the source. Artificial doubles use delayed copies: check the mix in mono for a hollow or phasey sound. Separate sung takes usually sound more natural.

    Fixed pitch intervals: +3 minor third, +4 major third, +7 fifth, −12 octave bass. These do not automatically follow your key or chords and can change vocal character. For descending lines, record the melody separately and choose it as a source. Mute excludes a layer; Solo includes only soloed layers.

    Quick sound

    Natural keeps the words clear, warms the guitar and adds a believable room around you.

    Personal sounds are stored in this browser. Start from your original recording to avoid applying effects twice. Noise reduction, de-essing, pitch and final loudness are applied only when saving the audio.

    Clean up

    The cover presets use no noise gate, so quiet guitar notes and natural string decay are kept. Leave hiss reduction off when the recording is already quiet.
